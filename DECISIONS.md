| 2026-11-07 | **AD-009: Strict EVM Causality Classification and Propagation Invariants** | Trace frames in OP Geth can catch internal errors (try/catch or low-level calls) and continue execution. When parent and child have distinct revert messages, both `rootRevertReason` and `nestedFailureReason` are preserved. | Forensic Architecture Lead |
